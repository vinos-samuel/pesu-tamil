# v1 Phrase List (Units 1–5, 46 phrases)

> Supersedes the unit sequencing in `curriculum.md` for v1 scope — v1 ships Units 1–5 below (46 phrases), not the full 12-unit sequence in the earlier draft. Unit 6+ moves to full sentences (see bottom).

⚠️ Draft, not verified — needs native-speaker review before recording. Romanized Tamil is for production reference only, **never shown on screen to the learner**.

## Unit 1 — Greetings & Courtesy (10)
1. Hello — *vanakkam*
2. How are you? (peer/cousin) — *eppadi irukka?*
3. How are you? (elder — Paati/Thatha) — *eppadi irukkeenga?* (honorific — own item, not a variant)
4. I'm fine — *nalla irukken*
5. Yes — *aamaa*
6. No — *illa*
7. Thank you — *nandri*
8. Sorry — *sorry* (often just borrowed) / *mannikkanum* (formal) ⚠️ confirm register
9. Goodbye — *poyttu varen* (lit. "I'll go and come" — not word-for-word)
10. Good night — *iravu vanakkam*

## Unit 2 — Family & Kinship (8 core + 2 bonus)
1. Mom — *amma*
2. Dad — *appa*
3. Grandma — *paati*
4. Grandpa — *thatha*
5. Older brother — *anna*
6. Older sister — *akka*
7. Younger brother — *thambi*
8. Younger sister — *thangachi*
9. (bonus) Mom's brother — *mama*
10. (bonus) Dad's sister — *athai* ⚠️ paternal-uncle elder/younger split deferred to v1.1

## Unit 3 — Mealtime Words (8)
1. I'm hungry — *pasikkudhu*
2. I want water — *thanni vaenum*
3. Give me water — *thanni kodu* ⚠️ direct, not "please pass"
4. I want to eat — *saapida vaenum*
5. Tastes good! — *rusiyaa irukku*
6. Is it spicy? — *kaaramaa irukka?*
7. I'm full / enough — *pothum*
8. A little more, please — *innum konjam*

## Unit 4 — Numbers 1–10 (10)
*onnu, rendu, moonu, naalu, anju, aaru, ezhu, ettu, onbadhu, pathu* — no register flags, stable across dialects.

## Unit 5 — Action Commands & Routines (10)
1. Come here — *inga vaa*
2. Sit down — *utkaar*
3. Eat — *saapdu*
4. Wake up — *ezhundhiru*
5. Sleep — *thoongu*
6. Wash your hands — *kai kazhuvu*
7. Brush your teeth — *"teeth" brush pannu* (Tanglish, commonly used) vs *pல் thei* (formal) ⚠️ decide on purpose
8. Let's go — *povom*
9. Wait — *nillu*
10. Be quiet — *satham podaadhey*
